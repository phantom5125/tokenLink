## Physical acceptance checklist

Use only the artifacts generated from source revision `d3b1cbc`. Record any
failure with the visible status text and the exported redacted diagnostics;
do not include peripheral UUIDs, task identifiers, tokens, or provider payloads.

1. Quit every running TokenLink instance before installing the candidate. Launch
   only the verified 0.2.2 app from `/Applications/TokenLink.app`, confirm there
   is exactly one TokenLink process, and confirm its executable resolves inside
   that bundle. Do not launch a worktree-built app or the legacy
   `io.github.phantom5125.tokenlink` bundle during acceptance.
2. Confirm TokenLink shows version 0.2.2 (5), the C152 System page shows
   `0.2.2-tokenlink`, and protocol v2 is active.
3. Flash `TokenLink-StopWatch-C152-0.2.2.bin` at offset `0x0` to the explicitly
   confirmed C152 port. Verify every esptool hash and observe a fresh
   `CODEX_MICRO_STOPWATCH_READY` marker after reset.
4. Launch TokenLink and open StopWatch without pressing Scan or Sync. Confirm
   that merely opening the page does not initialize CoreBluetooth or display a
   Bluetooth permission request.
5. Press Scan or Sync and complete the explicit authorization flow. Confirm the
   diagnostics distinguish permission, adapter, binding, connection progress,
   C04 notifications, last sync, and redacted failure state. Exercise the denied
   or disabled-adapter path once and verify the recovery guidance.
6. Export diagnostics and inspect the text before sharing. Confirm it contains
   no peripheral UUID, task ID, token, credential, or provider payload.
7. With more than three active Codex tasks, verify the watch shows the exact
   `N ACTIVE / 3 SHOWN` aggregate. Needs-input and failed tasks should outrank
   running tasks, and repeated refreshes should keep unchanged task slots stable.
8. Verify running, needs-input, complete, and failed rows remain distinguishable
   by shape/text as well as color: blue orbit, amber pulse, green check, and red
   alert. Check normal and dim brightness for clipping or excessive flicker.
9. Use the Mac-side focus control on every displayed row and confirm the exact
   Codex task becomes visible. Repeat from the matching C152 row through C04.
   Confirm TokenLink reports delivered, fallback, expired slot, unsupported
   provider, or Codex unavailable accurately without logging the task ID.
10. Quit Codex and retry focus, then restart Codex and retry. Confirm the visible
   outcome changes from unavailable/fallback to delivered and the intended task
   is selected.
11. Relaunch TokenLink, power-cycle the C152, and sleep/wake the Mac. Confirm the
    current binding reconnects, C04 resumes, the diagnostics advance, and no
    unexplained repeated authorization request appears.
12. Confirm TokenLink startup does not access `Claude Code-credentials`. Enable
    Claude explicitly and verify any Keychain prompt appears only at that point,
    explains the authorization scope, and identifies the application as
    TokenLink rather than a legacy bundle name.
13. Run a 15-30 minute interaction soak covering refresh, page changes, focus,
    reconnect, and dim/wake. Track the separate 24-hour power/battery soak as
    follow-up evidence if it remains outside the 0.2.2 release gate.

## Release decision

The source, versioned documentation, Mac archive, and C152 artifacts are ready
for user acceptance. Do not tag or publish the stable release until the exact
candidate has passed the physical flash and checklist above and the pushed
commit has passed hosted CI and review. The current evidence does not claim
notarization, Intel support, a Costs page, quota-SDK integration, or installer
work for 0.2.2.
