1. Open the packaged app, click the menu-bar item, visit all four routes, and quit
   from the menu.
2. If desired, enter user-owned Kimi, MiniMax, and GLM keys through the blank
   replacement fields, refresh once, and record only sanitized percentages.
3. With the existing firmware running, scan explicitly, select the intended
   device, bind it, sync Codex, and record discovery, binding, ATT ACK, and visible
   display update as four separate observations.

Until those steps are observed, they remain `NOT VERIFIED` regardless of build or
unit-test success.
