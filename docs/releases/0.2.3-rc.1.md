# TokenLink 0.2.3 RC 1

TokenLink 0.2.3 brings quota pace to the C152 watch face and introduces a
dedicated Cost Center on the Mac. This prerelease is intended for hands-on
review before the branch is merged and promoted to a stable release.

## Highlights

- Reworks the first watch page around the TokenLink open arc. The outer arc is
  quota, the clock sits inside it, and the lower area shows planned remaining
  quota, percentage-point variance, and reset countdown without duplicating a
  second `5h left` label.
- Adds Today, Week, and Month views to the Mac Cost Center while keeping
  provider-reported balances separate from local API-equivalent estimates.
- Aligns Codex estimates with the leading community method: request-level token
  deltas, disjoint input/cache buckets, recorded Fast tiers, request-level
  long-context pricing, and current reviewed GPT-5.6 prices.
- Shows calculation provenance, pricing coverage, excluded models, catalog
  version, and partial-scan warnings instead of silently treating unknown usage
  as free.
- Generates dedicated small-size Mac icon masters so the TokenLink open arc
  remains legible in Finder and sidebars instead of collapsing into the infinity
  mark.
- Retains the 0.2.2 session focus, lifecycle, Bluetooth rebind, and short
  power-button wake fixes already integrated on `main`.

## Review checklist

1. Compare the first watch page against the approved layout: quota outside,
   clock inside, and two compact information rows below.
2. Confirm Quota still shows every configured coding plan rather than only
   Codex.
3. Open Cost Center and switch Today, Week, and Month; confirm provider balances
   are not mixed with local API-equivalent estimates.
4. Expand a Codex calculation and verify input, cache read/write, output, model,
   speed, price source, and excluded-model notices are understandable.
5. Inspect the TokenLink app icon in Finder and a small macOS sidebar size.
6. On a C152, verify sleep/wake, reconnect, session focus, and quota refresh
   after flashing the matching 0.2.3 firmware candidate.

## Assets

- `TokenLink-0.2.3.dmg` and `.dmg.sha256`: Universal 2 Mac development build
  with the Applications shortcut.
- `TokenLink-StopWatch-C152-0.2.3.bin`: merged C152 image, flashed at offset
  `0x0` only after confirming the exact M5Stack StopWatch device port.
- `TokenLink-StopWatch-C152-0.2.3.zip`: split flash inputs, ELF, partition table,
  licenses, and image manifest.
- `firmware-manifest.json` and `SHA256SUMS`: release metadata and checksums for
  the firmware assets.

## Prerelease boundary

The Mac DMG attached to this RC is ad-hoc signed for development review. It is
not Developer ID signed, Apple-notarized, or a stable default download. The
stable tag remains fail-closed until signing and notarization credentials are
configured and the resulting artifact passes Gatekeeper verification.

Local Codex costs remain estimates at public API-equivalent rates. They are not
a ChatGPT/Codex subscription invoice, do not reduce plan quota, and may exclude
unknown or incompletely priced models. Project attribution, activity-duration
analytics, calendar heatmaps, and share cards are planned for the broader usage
analytics follow-up rather than this release candidate.
