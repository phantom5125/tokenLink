# TokenLink 0.2.0 RC 1

TokenLink 0.2 turns the Mac menu bar and M5Stack StopWatch C152 into one local
coding-plan surface.

## Highlights

- Four round-safe watch pages: Home, Quota, Sessions, and System.
- Codex, Claude, Kimi, MiniMax, and GLM quota in one native Mac app.
- Matching provider marks and quota bars on Mac and watch.
- Latest Codex task, aggregate active-task count, and up to three visible task
  rows on the watch.
- Tap-to-focus opens the matching Codex task on the Mac, with a compatibility
  activation fallback.
- Negotiated protocol v2 with byte-compatible protocol-v1 fallback.
- Configurable provider selection, theme, wake mode, and 12/24-hour time.
- Versioned, checksummed Mac archive with bundled resources and distribution
  notices.

## RC assets

- `TokenLink-0.2.0-macos-arm64.zip` and its `.sha256`: Apple Silicon,
  ad-hoc-signed development build.
- `TokenLink-StopWatch-C152-0.2.0-rc.1.bin` and its `.sha256`: combined image for
  M5Stack StopWatch C152 only, flashed at offset `0x0` after resolving and
  confirming the exact Espressif port.
- `TokenLink-StopWatch-C152-0.2.0-rc.1-source.zip` and its `.sha256`: exact
  PlatformIO source revision used to build the C152 image, distributed only
  through the TokenLink release.

## Install notes

- Mac-only users can build the app directly from the tagged source with
  `bash scripts/package_app.sh`. The attached arm64 archive is the same class of
  ad-hoc development build and may be rejected by Gatekeeper on another Mac.
- C152 users must verify the firmware checksum, identify and confirm the exact
  Espressif serial port, and flash the combined image at offset `0x0`. Do not use
  the image on another M5Stack model. See the repository Quick Start and
  [M5Stack factory recovery](https://docs.m5stack.com/en/guide/restore_factory/stopwatch).

The Mac archive is intentionally published as an RC development artifact. It is
not Developer ID signed, notarized, or stapled and must not be relabeled as the
default stable download. No Intel binary is included.

## Hardware evidence

- Firmware commit `0d5bf6c` was flashed to a confirmed C152 serial port; esptool
  verified every written hash and the watch emitted `CODEX_MICRO_STOPWATCH_READY`.
- The installed TokenLink 0.2.0 app negotiated protocol v2 and sent live Codex,
  Kimi, and MiniMax payloads. The aggregate active Codex count was two and the
  firmware received three named Codex work items.
