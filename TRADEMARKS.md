You may:

- truthfully state that your software uses, supports, or is based on TokenLink;
- link to the TokenLink repository and reproduce required attribution notices;
- redistribute an unmodified TokenLink build while retaining its original name,

- describe a community adapter as "for TokenLink" when the presentation makes

## Uses that require written permission

Please request permission before you:

- use `TokenLink` or a confusingly similar name as the primary name of a fork,
  product, company, domain, package, or service;
- use an official TokenLink logo as your product or organization logo;
- imply sponsorship, certification, partnership, or endorsement by TokenLink or
  its maintainer; or
- sell merchandise that uses official TokenLink branding.

Forks should use a distinct name and visual identity. A factual statement such
as "Based on TokenLink" is welcome when it is no more prominent than the fork's
own name and includes a link to this repository.
